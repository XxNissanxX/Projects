Punjabi food is known for its bold flavors, richness, and liberal use of dairy products like ghee, butter, and cream. Staples include wheat-based breads, such as naan, paratha, and kulcha, and dishes are often prepared using a tandoor oven, giving them a smoky flavor. Major dishes include butter chicken, dal makhani, and sarson ka saag, and the region also boasts a variety of sweets and popular beverages like lassi.
