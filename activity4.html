The Mughal Empire, which existed from 1526 to 1857, was founded by Babur and had its last emperor as Bahadur Shah II. It ruled over a vast territory that today forms parts of India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, and Afghanistan, with a population of around 125 million in 1595. The empire's official language was Persian, its state religion was Islam, and key capitals included Agra, Delhi, and Lahore.
